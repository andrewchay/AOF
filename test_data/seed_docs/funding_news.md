# Acme AI Raises Series A

Acme AI announced today that it has raised $15 million in Series A
funding led by Initialized Capital. Garry Tan, partner at Initialized,
will join the board of directors.

The funding will be used to expand the engineering team and accelerate
the development of the company's intelligent document processing platform.

Sarah Chen, CEO of Acme AI, said: "We are thrilled to partner with
Initialized Capital and Garry Tan to bring AI-powered document
understanding to every enterprise."
