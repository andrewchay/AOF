- case_id: PRC-2025-001
  title: "差旅费是否计入部门采购支出"
  domain: procurement
  metric: dept_procurement_spend
  dispute: |
    财务部定义的 dept_procurement_spend 包含差旅费（travel_expense），
    但采购部认为差旅费属于行政费用，不应计入采购支出。
    两个部门在月度经营会上对同一指标给出不同数字。
  context:
    baseline_value: 1250000
    finance_value: 1380000
    delta: 130000
    delta_pct: 10.4
    data_sources: [erp_ap, expense_system, travel_platform]
    period: "2025-Q2"
  resolution:
    decided_by: cfo
    decision: "差旅费单独列示为 admin_travel_spend，不计入 dept_procurement_spend"
    rationale: "差旅费审批流与采购审批流不同，合并统计导致责任边界模糊"
    new_metric_created: admin_travel_spend
  outcome: validated
  roles_involved: [finance_analyst, procurement_manager, cfo]
  tags: [metric-definition, scope-dispute, cross-department]

- case_id: PRC-2025-002
  title: "紧急采购是否适用标准审批阈值"
  domain: procurement
  metric: emergency_purchase_approval_rate
  dispute: |
    标准采购审批阈值：>50K 需总监审批，>200K 需VP审批。
    紧急采购（emergency_purchase）在 2025-Q1 有 23 笔超过 50K，
    其中 15 笔走了事后补批，采购部认为这是合理例外，
    内审认为这绕过了审批控制。
  context:
    baseline_value: 0.05
    actual_value: 0.65
    delta: 0.60
    delta_pct: 1200.0
    data_sources: [procurement_system, approval_log]
    period: "2025-Q1"
  resolution:
    decided_by: cfo
    decision: "紧急采购>50K 必须在 48h 内完成补批，否则触发例外升级"
    rationale: "保留紧急采购灵活性，但增加时间约束和追溯要求"
    policy_updated: emergency_purchase_sla
  outcome: validated
  roles_involved: [procurement_manager, internal_audit, cfo]
  tags: [approval-threshold, exception-handling, policy-gap]

- case_id: PRC-2025-003

  domain: procurement
  metric: monthly_procurement_spend
  dispute: |

    但货物可能 3 个月后才到。财务部认为应按权责发生制分摊，
    采购部认为预付款是现金流支出，应计入当月。
    导致月度采购支出波动异常大。

    baseline_value: 800000
    finance_value: 950000
    delta: 150000
    delta_pct: 18.75
    data_sources: [erp_ap, contract_system]
    period: "2025-03"
  resolution:
    decided_by: cfo
    decision: "预付款单独列示为 prepayment_outflow，按月摊销计入 procurement_spend"
    rationale: "区分现金流口径和权责口径，避免单月波动误导决策"
    new_metric_created: prepayment_outflow
  outcome: validated
  roles_involved: [finance_analyst, procurement_manager, cfo]
  tags: [accrual-vs-cash, timing-difference, metric-split]

# Procurement Caliber Governance — Semantic Resources
# 定义采购支出控制领域的指标、动作、工作流和策略

# ========== Metrics ==========
# 采购支出核心指标，每个指标有明确的口径定义

- resource_id: aof://acme/finance/metric/monthly_procurement_spend
  kind: Metric
  name: monthly_procurement_spend
  domain: finance
  owner: finance-team
  spec:
    description: "月度采购支出（执行口径）"
    formula: "SUM(actual_payment_amount) WHERE payment_status = 'completed' AND month = period"
    unit: CNY
    frequency: monthly
    caliber_definition: |
      执行口径：以实际付款完成为准。
      不含：预付款、押金、内部转移、样品采购。
    data_sources: [erp_ap, procurement_system]
    dimensions: [department, month]
    owner_role: finance_analyst
    approval_required: true
    version: 1.0.0

- resource_id: aof://acme/finance/metric/budget_vs_actual_variance
  kind: Metric
  name: budget_vs_actual_variance
  domain: finance
  owner: finance-team
  spec:
    description: "预算 vs 实际差异"
    formula: "(actual_spend - budget_amount) / budget_amount"
    unit: ratio
    frequency: monthly
    caliber_definition: |
      预算按承诺口径，核算按执行口径。
      差异分析用于预算控制。
    data_sources: [budget_system, erp_ap]
    dimensions: [department, month]
    owner_role: finance_analyst
    approval_required: false
    version: 1.0.0

- resource_id: aof://acme/finance/metric/framework_contract_spend
  kind: Metric
  name: framework_contract_spend
  domain: finance
  owner: finance-team
  spec:
    description: "框架协议支出（已废弃，拆分为 commitment 和 execution）"
    formula: "SUM(contract_amount) WHERE contract_type = 'framework'"
    unit: CNY
    frequency: monthly
    caliber_definition: |
      已废弃：拆分为 framework_commitment（承诺口径）和 framework_execution（执行口径）。
    data_sources: [contract_system, procurement_system]
    dimensions: [supplier, month]
    owner_role: procurement_manager
    approval_required: true
    version: 1.0.0

  kind: Policy

      emergency_changes:

        required_approvals: [cfo]
        sla_hours: 48
      dispute_escalation:
        max_attempts: 2
        escalate_to: [ceo]
        sla_hours: 24
